The Banking Semantic Layer models your institution's financial product catalog as a set of structured, queryable entities, giving you a semantic bridge between the products your institution offers and the customers and accounts that consume them. The `bsl:Product` entity acts as the canonical representation of any financial product — from a basic savings account offering to a structured investment instrument — and connects to customers, accounts, and regulatory classifications through a consistent set of typed relationships.

## Product Model

The `bsl:Product` entity describes a financial product as it exists in the product catalog, independent of any specific customer subscription. Think of it as the template: it defines the product's characteristics, pricing parameters, regulatory classification, and issuing institution. When a customer subscribes to a product, the `bsl:subscribedTo` relationship links their `bsl:Customer` entity to the relevant `bsl:Product`, while the resulting `bsl:Account` entity captures the live operational state of that subscription.

## Product Family Taxonomy

The `bsl:ProductFamily` vocabulary organizes products into five top-level families, enabling high-level filtering and reporting across your catalog.

| Family | Description |
|---|---|
| `bsl:Deposits` | Deposit-taking products including savings accounts, term deposits, and certificates of deposit |
| `bsl:Loans` | Lending products such as personal loans, auto loans, and overdraft facilities |
| `bsl:Cards` | Payment card products including credit cards, debit cards, and prepaid cards |
| `bsl:Investments` | Investment and wealth management products such as mutual funds, brokerage accounts, and structured products |
| `bsl:Insurance` | Insurance and protection products offered through the institution or bancassurance partnerships |

## Product Properties

| Property | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bsl:productId` | `string` | Yes | Unique product identifier within the catalog |
| `bsl:productName` | `string` | Yes | Human-readable product name as used in customer-facing materials |
| `bsl:productFamily` | `bsl:ProductFamily` | Yes | Product family classification from the `bsl:ProductFamily` taxonomy |
| `bsl:interestRate` | `decimal` | No | Annual interest rate expressed as a decimal (e.g., `0.045` for 4.5%) |
| `bsl:currency` | `bsl:Currency` | No | Base currency of the product, as an ISO 4217 code |
| `bsl:minBalance` | `decimal` | No | Minimum balance required to open or maintain the product |
| `bsl:maxLimit` | `decimal` | No | Maximum credit or loan limit applicable to this product |
| `bsl:term` | `integer` | No | Product term in months, applicable to fixed-term products such as loans and term deposits |
| `bsl:issuedBy` | `bsl:Institution` | Yes | The financial institution that issues or administers this product |
| `bsl:regulatoryCategory` | `bsl:RegulatoryCategory` | No | Regulatory classification (e.g., `bsl:RetailDeposit`, `bsl:ConsumerCredit`) for compliance reporting |

## Example: Product Record in JSON-LD

The following example shows a savings product entry in JSON-LD format, ready for loading into the semantic catalog.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://ontology.bankingsl.io/v1/context.json",
  "@type": "bsl:Product",
  "bsl:productId": "PRD-SAV-001",
  "bsl:productName": "Premier Savings Account",
  "bsl:productFamily": "bsl:Deposits",
  "bsl:interestRate": 0.045,
  "bsl:currency": "USD",
  "bsl:minBalance": 500.00,
  "bsl:regulatoryCategory": "bsl:RetailDeposit",
  "bsl:issuedBy": {
    "@type": "bsl:Institution",
    "bsl:institutionId": "INST-001",
    "bsl:institutionName": "First National Bank"
  }
}
```

## Product–Customer Linkage

The `bsl:subscribedTo` property links a `bsl:Customer` entity to one or more `bsl:Product` entities, representing the set of products to which that customer currently has an active relationship. You can traverse this relationship in both directions — finding all products a customer holds, or finding all customers subscribed to a specific product.

The query below retrieves all customers who are subscribed to at least one product in the `bsl:Loans` family, which is a common starting point for credit risk portfolio analysis.

```sparql
SELECT ?customer ?product WHERE {
  ?customer a bsl:Customer ;
            bsl:subscribedTo ?product .
  ?product bsl:productFamily bsl:Loans .
}
```

You can extend this query to include `bsl:interestRate`, `bsl:maxLimit`, or `bsl:riskTier` to build a richer view of your loan portfolio alongside customer risk profiles.

<Info>
Your product catalog does not need to be manually maintained in the semantic layer. The Banking Semantic Layer ships with integration connectors for major core banking platforms — including Temenos Transact, FIS Modern Banking Platform, and Finastra Fusion — that can automatically sync product definitions on a scheduled or event-driven basis. When a new product is launched or an existing product's terms are updated in your core banking system, the connector propagates those changes to the semantic catalog, keeping `bsl:Product` entities current without manual intervention. See the Integration Connectors guide for setup instructions.
</Info>
